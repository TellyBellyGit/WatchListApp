Stock strength guide

Tick what you can see on the chart. The guide answers two separate questions: what condition is the stock in, and is there a sensible trade right now.

A structured decision aid. It does not predict the next candle and is not a statistically proven system. Test the gates against past trades and adjust them to suit you.

Quick rules
  • Strong: above VWAP, 9/21 EMAs aligned and rising, higher highs and lows, volume on advances, no blow-off signs, 5-minute agrees.
  • Weak: unconfirmed (early, wait), deteriorating (was strong, avoid or exit), conflicted (signals disagree).
  • Dead: structure broken and the recovery attempt failed.
  • Bullish signals are not votes. Gates cap the rating: below VWAP with lost EMAs, blow-off signs, or a 5-minute conflict cannot be Strong.
  • A Strong stock can still be a poor trade: extended, resistance too close, or reward below your minimum.
  • A level that has rejected price before is supply until it is cleared on volume.
  • No stop that proves you wrong means no trade.
