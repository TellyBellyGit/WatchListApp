TOS Active Trader Setup & Rules

MICRO-CAP CRITICAL
01
Switch Offsets from `%` to `$` (Absolute Cents)

On $1.00 stocks, a 2% stop is only 2 cents ($0.02). Normal bid-ask spread noise will wick you out instantly before the move develops. Set active trader buttons to cent offsets (e.g. +0.10 / -0.05).

02
3-Cent Spread Gatekeeper Rule

Never hit BUY ASK if the spread is wider than 3 cents ($0.03). Crossing a 4¢ spread on a $1.00 stock means you start immediately down -4.0% on execution!

03
Trailing Stop Warning in Fast Markets

Trailing stops execute as Market Orders when triggered. In low-float halts or fast market dumps, a 5¢ trailing stop can suffer 3¢ to 5¢ negative slippage. Use fixed limit targets for exits.
