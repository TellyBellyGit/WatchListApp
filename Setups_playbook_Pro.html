Professional Setup Playbook

1. Uptrend Pullback Setup

High Probability
Entry Trigger & Mechanics

Price establishes a clear impulsive higher high, followed by a controlled low-volume pullback into the rising 9 or 20 Exponential Moving Average (EMA). Entry is executed on the close of a bullish reversal candlestick (e.g., hammer or bullish engulfing) right at the dynamic support zone.

Stop Loss Placement

Placed strictly below the swing low of the pullback candle or just under the recent structural swing low with an ATR buffer to avoid market noise and fakeouts.

Profit Targets (TP1 & TP2)

TP1: Scale out 50% at the previous swing high (1.5R).
TP2: Trail remainder along the 9 EMA or target the 1.618 Fibonacci extension of the pullback wave (2.5R - 3.5R).

Volume & Market Psychology

Volume must systematically dry up (decline) during the pullback phase, indicating lack of aggressive selling pressure. A surge of volume on the trigger can show increased participation, but it does not identify the participants or guarantee continuation.

Risk protocol — before every order

1. Define the loss firstShares = floor((account equity × risk fraction) ÷ (entry price − stop price)). Reduce size further for spread, slippage, halts or unusually fast movement. The formula is a ceiling, not a promise that the stop will fill at its price.
2. Check tradeabilityInspect bid–ask spread, displayed liquidity, recent dollar volume, volatility and any news/halts. A technically attractive chart can still be a poor trade if the exit is difficult or costs consume the expected move.
3. Honour invalidationSet the structural stop before entry; do not widen it to avoid taking a loss. If the setup fails, exit according to the plan. Never rely on a hoped-for bounce to rescue a broken thesis.
