Five-Point Strength Test

Intraday Stock Assessment Framework

Intraday Edge

Eliminating Noise & Emotional Decision-Making

Assessing the true strength of a stock price during intraday trading requires more than just chasing momentum. This rigorous 5-point filter evaluates structure, holding power, volume, trend alignment, and opportunity to classify stocks into STRONG, WEAK, or DEAD categories.

TEST 01

1. Price Structure

Is it actually climbing?

  • ● Strong: Higher highs and higher lows. Clean upward trajectory.
  • ● Weak: Sideways, choppy or struggling to make progress.
  • ● Dead: Lower highs and lower lows after the upward move fails.
TEST 02

2. Holding Power

Does it keep its gains?

  • ● Strong: Stocks hold pullbacks and previous breakout levels.
  • ● Weak: Stocks repeatedly surrender gains.
  • ● Note: A stock that spikes and falls back to its starting price has not demonstrated sustained strength.
TEST 03

3. Volume

Is the price movement supported?

  • ● Constructive: Rising price with expanding volume. Healthy pullback on declining volume.
  • ● Unconfirmed: Rising price on unusually thin volume (not automatically strong).
  • ● Warning: Falling price on expanding volume.
TEST 04

4. Trend (VWAP & EMAs)

Are technical anchors supporting the move?

  • ● Strong: Price holds above VWAP, 9 EMA > 21 EMA (both ideally rising).
  • ● Weak: Price repeatedly crosses VWAP; EMAs are flat or tangled.
  • ● Deteriorating: Price loses VWAP, EMAs turn down, recovery attempts fail.
TEST 05

5. Opportunity & Execution

Is there a sensible trade setup?

Avoid Chasing

Even if the stock is strong, don't chase an extended move. Look for a pullback, retest, or continuation entry.

Risk & Resistance

Identify where the trade is invalidated (stop loss) and check that there is enough room to the next major resistance.

The Decision in One Sentence

Strong

Climbing, holding gains, and supported by convincing price-and-volume behaviour.

Weak

Unconvincing progress, thin-volume rise, choppy structure or conflicting evidence.

Dead

The upward move has failed, support has broken, and selling is dominating recovery attempts.

Golden Rule / Qualification: Thin volume alone does not make a stock weak, and losing VWAP alone does not make it dead. Always judge the combined evidence across all pillars before making your trade decision.
