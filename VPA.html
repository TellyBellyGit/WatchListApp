Market Intent, Wyckoff Structural Cycles, Single-Candle Anomalies & Multi-Bar Sequences

Block 1: Wyckoff Market Cycle & Single-Candle VPA
Wyckoff Market Cycle & Synchronized Volume Map Price Action (Top) vs. Volume Effort (Bottom)

1. Accumulation Phase

Smart money absorbs panic selling after a prolonged drop to build long inventory.

  • Selling Climax: Ultra-high panic volume spike.
  • Stopping Volume: Massive volume absorbs downward flow.
  • Supply Test: Flat, dead volume confirms no remaining sellers.

2. Markup Phase

Path of least resistance is upward. Public joins as trend establishes.

  • Validation: Wide spread + rising volume staircase.
  • Low-Volume Pullbacks: Shallow dips on dead volume equal safe re-entries.

3. Distribution Phase

Smart money offloads inventory into retail buyer FOMO near peak prices.

  • Buying Climax: Vertical volume explosion at high.
  • The Squat: Massive volume effort, zero price push (heavy limit sells).

4. Markdown Phase

Supply overpowers demand. Price cascades down rapidly as stops are hit.

  • Hollow Moves: Upward bounces on dead volume (dead-cat traps).
  • Heavy Down-Bars: Strong selling validated by persistent volume.
ⓘ VPA Terminology Note: What is "Spread"?
In classical VPA, "Spread" refers to the total price range between the High and Low of the candle (the entire vertical distance fought over), not just the body (Open to Close). Measuring High-to-Low is vital because long wicks represent price rejection, allowing you to catch Stopping Volume, Climax setups, and Absorption even when the candle body itself is narrow.

Validation (Healthy Trend)
Marubozu / Trend Bar Wide body, tiny wicks + strong volume
Wide spread, closes near the high/low of the candle. Steady, progressively rising volume. Effort equals Result: Increased volume confirms real buying or selling. Genuine institutional participation supporting the direction.
Buying Climax (Top)
Shooting Star / Upper Wick Long upper wick + massive vertical volume spike
Wide spread or long upper wick after a prolonged move up. Ultra-high vertical spike in volume. Climax: Extreme effort offloaded into retail buyer FOMO. Professionals are distributing (selling) their positions at the peak.
Selling Climax (Bottom)
Hammer / Bullish Pin Bar Deep lower tail + massive panic volume spike
Wide spread downward, often followed by long lower wicks. Ultra-high vertical spike in volume. Climax: Extreme effort absorbing panic-selling retail orders. Professionals are accumulating (buying) at a major discount.
The Squat
Doji / Tiny Spinning Top Micro high-to-low range + huge volume bar
Very narrow price spread (small candle body). Massive / High volume. Anomaly (High Effort, Low Result): Volume is huge, but price refuses to move. Market Makers have parked giant limit orders, absorbing all flow. Reversal imminent.
Hollow Move (Trap)
Wide Range "Ghost" Bar Big candle range on near-zero volume
Wide spread candle moving strongly up or down. Low / Below average volume. Anomaly (Low Effort, High Result): Price moves easily without real financial backing. Unbacked move (lack of institutional interest). Likely to stall or reverse quickly.
Stopping Volume
Absorption Tail / Hammer Deep lower wick at low + huge volume
Narrow spread with a deep lower wick (hammer/pin bar). Ultra-high volume at the end of a downtrend. Anomaly (High Effort, Low Result): Heavy selling met by equal institutional buying. Institutions have slammed the brakes on a drop. A floor is being established.
Test of Supply
Low-Volume Dip Narrow red/green bar + flat volume
Small downward dip, narrow spread. Very low / Flat volume. Validation: Low effort yields minimal downward movement. Confirms no active sellers remain in the market. Green light for an upward move.
Test of Demand
Low-Volume Probe Narrow upward push + flat volume
Small upward push, narrow spread. Very low / Flat volume. Validation: Low effort yields minimal upward movement. Confirms no active buyers remain at higher levels. Green light for a drop.
Block 2: Multi-Bar Sequence Blueprint

The Staircase Ideal Ignition

  • Price: Expanding green spreads with higher highs and higher lows.
  • Volume: Sloping upward harmoniously candle-over-candle.
  • VPA Law: Effort validates Result across sequence.
Verdict: High-Conviction Trend Entry

Breakout & Retest The Launchpad

  • Price: Heavy breakout bar above resistance, followed by shallow pullbacks.
  • Volume: Massive volume on breakout; collapses to near zero on pullback.
  • VPA Law: Zero selling effort proves overhead supply is cleared.
Verdict: Low-Risk Re-entry Zone

Climax & Squat Exhaustion

  • Price: Vertical green spike -> micro-body/doji -> engulfing red bar.
  • Volume: Monster spikes on both the climax and narrow squat bar.
  • VPA Law: Extreme effort with zero price progress = massive absorption.
Verdict: Exit Immediately / Do Not Chase

Hollow Rally False Spark

  • Price: Price drifts upward with wide green candles.
  • Volume: Flatlining or declining well below the 20-period average.
  • VPA Law: Low effort yielding high result = unbacked ghost rally.
Verdict: High-Risk Trap / Ignore Entry
Sequence Pattern Price Trajectory Volume Progression VPA Law Analysis Execution Strategy
The Staircase Consecutive higher highs & higher lows with steady spread expansion. Steadily increasing bar-over-bar ($Vol_1 < Vol_2 < Vol_3$). Validation: Effort matches Result. Real institutional buying pushing ask levels. Join early on candle 2-3 or wait for first low-volume pullback. High win rate setup.
Breakout & Retest Wide green breakout candle followed by 2-3 narrow red/doji pullback candles. Huge volume spike on breakout bar; volume drops off a cliff on pullbacks. Supply Exhaustion: High effort breaks key level; near-zero effort on dip confirms no remaining sellers. Enter near the retest support level as soon as a turn candle appears on rising volume.
Compression to Expansion 3-5 narrow, tight bars (the Squeeze) followed by an explosive directional bar. Volume flatlines near zero during compression, then explodes on the breakout candle. Coiled Spring: Low effort/volatility shifts rapidly into maximum energy release. Trigger entry on the breakout bar when volume crosses above average.
Climax & Absorption Parabolic vertical green candle ending in a small body/doji near the high. Extreme volume spike on the green bar, followed by equal/higher volume on the narrow bar. Anomaly (The Squat): Enormous effort with zero price progress. Market makers absorbing all bids. Do not enter long under any circumstances. Tighten stops or take profits immediately.
The Ghost Rally Price appears to be rallying nicely with medium-to-wide green bodies. Volume is declining or trapped below the 20-period moving average line. Anomaly (Hollow Move): Price moving easily due to lack of asks, not real institutional demand. Ignore the movement. Expect a sharp rejection as soon as a real seller appears.
Key Execution Rules for Multi-Bar Analysis
  • Rule of 3 Candles: A single candle suggests intent, two candles indicate potential, but three candles establish a validated sequence.
  • Volume Slope Threshold: In a true Staircase or ignition sequence, volume must exceed the 20-period moving average by the second bar of the sequence.
  • The Pullback Delta: A healthy pullback should see volume drop by at least 50% relative to the breakout bar's volume bar height.

High Volume Node (HVN) / VPOC Price enters heavy historical transaction zone or major volume shelf. Massive volume expansion; bids/asks absorb huge order flow. Heavy Friction Zone: Breakout requires Ultra-High RVOL to clear resting supply. Rejection on high volume confirms institutional defence. Require RVOL > 2.5x to trade breakouts through HVNs.

Low Resistance Glide: Do not misinterpret low volume in an LVN as a "Ghost Trap." Price moves freely because few orders exist to slow it down. Valid Speed Zone - Ride momentum to next HVN.
Key Support / VWAP / EMA Bounce Price pulls back to test dynamic or horizontal support. Volume dries up completely on the approach, then spikes on the bounce candle. Supply Exhaustion: Low volume on the dip proves sellers are gone; sudden volume surge on turning bar confirms institutional buyers defending. High-Conviction Re-Entry on volume surge candle.
Resistance Breakout Failure Price attempts to push past session high or major resistance level. Volume spikes heavily, but candle body stalls or leaves long upper wick. Institutional Overhead Defense: Large limit sell orders absorbing market buy orders. High effort with zero progress at key level. Invalidated Breakout - Stand down or take profit.
